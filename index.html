A closer look

Behind the implementation

Keeping historical payment calculations consistent

The challenge

Payment deduction rates vary by clinic and patient referral source. Applying updated rates to older payments would change historical financial records.

The implementation

I preserved the deduction rate applied to each payment, rather than relying only on the current rate configuration. I also implemented payment corrections and cancellations with an audit trail.

The result

Later rate changes leave historical payment calculations intact, while corrections and cancellations remain traceable.
